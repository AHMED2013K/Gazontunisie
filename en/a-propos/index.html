About

Gazon Tunisie, a Tunisian garden center based in Nabeul

Gazon Tunisie supports homeowners and professionals with Paspalum lawn, American lawn and decorative palm trees for outdoor projects.

Our activity

Based in Grombalia, Gazon Tunisie delivers natural rolled lawn and decorative palm trees across Tunisia, with delivery fees calculated for your address and confirmed before you order.

We keep the offer focused: two natural lawn types and decorative palm trees, with a quote based on area, city and delivery conditions.

Our approach

We favour fast, direct contact via WhatsApp to answer your questions, provide a delivery quote or advise you on a variety suited to your region and project.
