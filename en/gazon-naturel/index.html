Natural lawn

Natural lawn in Tunisia: varieties, price and care

Natural lawn remains the preferred choice for creating a greener, fresher and more pleasant garden in Tunisia. To choose well, you need to consider the climate, soil type, surface area and budget. Gazon Tunisie helps you compare options, estimate the cost per m² and prepare your order with confidence.

What is rolled natural lawn?

Rolled natural lawn is grown in open ground and then cut into slabs with its roots and a thin layer of soil before being delivered and laid directly on prepared ground. Unlike seeding, it provides an immediate green result.

In Tunisia, the varieties selected are chosen for their heat resistance, their ability to remain dense even in dry summers, and their suitability for villas, pool areas and outdoor spaces.

Is natural lawn suitable for the Tunisian climate?

Yes, provided you choose the right variety and follow a few good practices:

  • Water early in the morning or in the evening to limit evaporation in summer
  • Mow regularly without cutting too short to preserve soil freshness
  • Plan for more watering during the first two weeks after laying
  • Adjust the watering frequency by region (more often in Sfax or Gabès than in Bizerte)

Frequently asked questions about natural lawn

Does natural lawn need a lot of water in Tunisia?

Water needs are higher in hot, dry southern regions and lower in the north. Watering adapted to the season helps reduce consumption.

How long does natural lawn take to root?

Usually between 2 and 3 weeks, provided regular watering is maintained during that period.

Can you walk on natural lawn immediately after laying?

It is best to wait 2 to 3 weeks before heavy use, to let the roots establish properly in the soil.
