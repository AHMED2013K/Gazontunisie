Natural lawn at Gazon Tunisie

Whether for a villa garden, pool area, private plot or professional green space, natural rolled lawn delivers an immediate and elegant result, with better comfort than bare soil.

At Gazon Tunisie, we help you choose the right lawn based on your surface area, sun exposure, soil conditions and budget. We explain the price per m², delivery charges and the best option before you order.

Why choose our natural lawn?

  • Clear pricing that decreases with total area ordered

  • Advice on choice, soil preparation and installation
  • Delivery across Tunisia depending on your region
  • Fast support via WhatsApp and a personalized quote

Two lawn types, one price

Gazon Tunisie offers two natural rolled lawn options: American lawn and Paspalum lawn. Pricing starts at 9 DT/sqm for 50 sqm or more; below 50 sqm, the price is quoted on request.

The 50 sqm threshold applies to the total surface area ordered, regardless of the type chosen. Our team advises you based on usage, region and maintenance level.

Frequently asked questions about natural lawn in Tunisia

What is the lawn price in Tunisia at Gazon Tunisie?

Pricing starts at 9 DT/sqm for 50 sqm or more. For areas under 50 sqm, the lawn and delivery prices are quoted according to your address.

Which lawn type should I choose: American or Paspalum?

American lawn is often preferred for dense coverage and strong sun resistance. Paspalum stands out for its finer leaves and good adaptation to the Tunisian climate. We recommend the best option according to your project.

Is the lawn delivered ready to lay?

Yes. The lawn is delivered in rolls ready to install on prepared soil. We recommend laying it quickly after receiving it for better rooting.

Do you deliver across Tunisia?

Yes. We deliver to most cities and regions of Tunisia, with delivery costs tailored to your area and the quantity ordered.
