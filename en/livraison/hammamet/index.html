Hammamet is part of Nabeul governorate, where our depot is located: delivery fees there are among the lowest in our network. The exact amount is calculated for your address and confirmed before you order.

Do you offer palm trees suited to hotels and tourist complexes?

Yes, the Gazon Tunisie decorative palm tree at 500 DT/m is a common choice for driveways, pools and gardens of tourist complexes in Hammamet, and delivery fees there are calculated for your address, including for bulk orders.
