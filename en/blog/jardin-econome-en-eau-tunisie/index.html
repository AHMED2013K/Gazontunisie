Creating a water-efficient garden suited to the Tunisian climate

Published on 22 July 2026

Faced with hot, dry summers, limiting a garden's water use has become a common concern in Tunisia. Here is how to achieve it without sacrificing aesthetics.

Good habits for saving water

  • Water early morning or evening to limit evaporation
  • Mulch flower beds to retain soil moisture longer
  • Group plants by their water needs for more targeted watering
  • Favour deep, less frequent watering over short daily watering on established shrubs

Does natural lawn have a place in a water-efficient garden?

Yes, provided you adapt watering frequency to the season and your region, and avoid mowing too short, which helps the soil retain moisture longer.
